# Brand assets

## Official FCCLA logo (required for nav)

1. Sign in to the National FCCLA Portal or open the branding guidelines:
   https://fcclainc.org/communications/branding-guidelines/fccla-logos
2. Download the official **Emblem** or **Tagline** logo PNG (transparent preferred).
3. Save it here as:

   `fccla-logo.png`

Do not recreate or redraw the mark. Until the file exists, the site shows a temporary FCCLA text badge in the nav.
